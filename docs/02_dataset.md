## 1. Introduction

Training a Large Language Model (LLM) begins with selecting a high-quality dataset. The quality of the training data directly influences the model's ability to understand language, learn meaningful patterns, generate coherent text, and generalize to unseen inputs.

For this project, we use the **TinyStories** dataset, which is specifically designed for training and evaluating small language models efficiently.

TinyStories is a synthetic text dataset consisting of millions of short stories written using simple English vocabulary and grammar. The stories are generated by advanced language models following carefully designed prompts that encourage logical reasoning, consistency, creativity, and coherent storytelling.

Due to hardware limitations, the complete TinyStories dataset was not used. Instead, a subset of **10,000 stories** was selected for both tokenizer training and GPT-1 model training. This subset is sufficient for demonstrating the complete language model development pipeline while keeping computational requirements manageable.

### Dataset Characteristics

* Millions of short, coherent stories
* Simple and easy-to-understand English vocabulary
* Grammatically correct text
* Minimal noisy or harmful content
* Ideal for educational projects and LLM experimentation

---

# 2. Why TinyStories?

TinyStories offers several advantages over traditional internet-scale datasets.

## 2.1 Small Yet Effective

Compared to large-scale datasets such as **Common Crawl** or **The Pile**, TinyStories contains significantly fewer tokens, making it practical to train language models on consumer-grade hardware.

---

## 2.2 High Data Quality

Since the dataset is synthetically generated and carefully curated, it contains very little noise, duplicated content, malformed HTML, or irrelevant text that is commonly found in web-scraped datasets.

This allows the model to focus on learning meaningful language patterns rather than handling noisy data.

---

## 2.3 Faster Experimentation

The relatively small dataset size enables rapid experimentation with different components of a language model, including:

* Transformer architectures
* Hyperparameter tuning
* Tokenization strategies
* Training pipelines
* Inference techniques

without requiring weeks of computation.

---

## 2.4 Ideal for Learning

TinyStories has become a popular dataset for educational projects because it enables developers to understand the complete LLM development pipeline without requiring expensive computational infrastructure.

Since the primary objective of this repository is to implement GPT-1 from scratch rather than reproduce state-of-the-art performance, TinyStories provides an excellent balance between dataset quality and computational efficiency.

---

# 3. Dataset Subsampling

The original TinyStories dataset contains millions of stories. Training GPT-1 on the complete dataset would require significantly more computational resources, storage capacity, and training time than were available for this project.

Therefore, a subset of **10,000 stories** was randomly selected and used throughout the entire implementation.

The selected subset was used for:

* Training the Byte Pair Encoding (BPE) tokenizer
* Creating the tokenized corpus
* Training the GPT-1 language model
* Evaluating the complete training pipeline

Although using a smaller subset limits the language understanding capabilities of the final model, it is more than sufficient for demonstrating every major component involved in building a GPT-style language model from scratch.

---

# 4. Data Cleaning

Although TinyStories is already a well-curated dataset, a lightweight preprocessing pipeline was applied before tokenization.

The preprocessing steps include:

1. Removing leading and trailing whitespace.
2. Normalizing line endings to `\n`.
3. Removing empty stories.
4. Collapsing multiple consecutive blank lines.

These preprocessing steps ensure that the input text is clean and consistent before training the tokenizer and GPT model.

# 6. Train / Validation Split

After preprocessing, the selected stories were divided into training and validation sets.

| Split      | Percentage |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| Training   | **80%**    |
| Validation | **20%**    |


The **training set** is used to optimize the GPT-1 model parameters, while the **validation set** is used to monitor model performance during training and to identify the best-performing checkpoint.

---

# 7. Summary

TinyStories is an excellent dataset for training small language models because it combines high-quality synthetic text with manageable computational requirements.

By selecting a subset of **10,000 stories**, applying a lightweight preprocessing pipeline, and splitting the data into training and validation sets, the project establishes a clean and efficient foundation for implementing every stage of the GPT-1 pipeline—from tokenizer training and data preparation to model training, inference, and evaluation.

Although the resulting model is considerably smaller than production-scale language models, the dataset is sufficiently representative to demonstrate the core principles behind modern autoregressive language models.
